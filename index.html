HOW TO PLAY

How to Play Drawful

1

Draw the prompt

Each player gets a strange phrase or title on their phone. Draw it using your finger or device. There is no eraser, so do your best.

2

Write a fake title

Once drawings are done, everyone looks at a drawing on the main screen. On your phone, type a fake title that acts as a decoy.

3

Guess the real title

All the decoy titles and the real title appear on the screen. Vote on what you think the true title is.

4

Score points

You get points if you guess the correct title, and you get bonus points when other players pick your fake decoy title.
